Legacy of Excellence

About Our Institution

Nurturing minds, building character, and fostering a tradition of academic brilliance for generations to come.

expand_more

S. S. Agrawal Public School, Navsari, managed by Agrawal Education Foundation, is a dream project of Late Shri Shivcharanlalji Agrawal.

Our institution stands as a testament to the power of vision and the commitment to educational equity. We believe in providing a holistic environment where every student is encouraged to explore their unique potential while staying grounded in strong moral values.

The foundation ensures that quality education remains accessible, focusing on state-of-the-art facilities and a curriculum that balances traditional wisdom with contemporary global standards.

Our Founder & Visionary

Late Shri Shivcharanlalji Agrawal

"Education is not just the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire."

Shri Shivcharanlalji Agrawal's legacy continues to illuminate our path. His belief that education should empower individuals to think critically and act compassionately remains at the heart of our mission.

Under his guidance, the school was conceived not just as an academic center, but as a crucible for character building and societal transformation.
